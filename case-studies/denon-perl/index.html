Denon headphones

My role was to redesign the app for the Denon PerL Pro — UX, UI, animations, and flows.

Overview

Overview

After acquiring Nura, there was a need to redesign the app to accommodate the new requirements for the Denon brand identity and app experience.

How we got there

Process

Understand

User testing sessions to uncover major and minor pain points in the existing, re-skinned Nura app.

Analyse

After analysing user testing results and researching online feedback on the existing app, all major and minor points of improvement were listed.

Identify, prioritise & refine

Changes were labelled, prioritised and taken into action.

Initiate

Major flows such as sign up, setup, and profile creation were created.

Refine & define

After aligning with internal stakeholders, screens were created, tested, and built into a flow.

Validate

Finalising designs, adding last touches, creating animations.

Finalise

Design files cleaned up, ensuring all screens and components are integrated into the design system and ready for development.

User testing

Understand

App audit, followed by user testing sessions with 8 people to uncover pains, needs, and areas of improvement on the existing app.

Key findings

Analyse

  • Connection and Pairing Difficulties: A significant number of users struggled with the device pairing process. Switching between Bluetooth settings and the app caused confusion, leading users to be uncertain about their connection status.
  • Progress Feedback During Profile Creation: The loading indicator during profile setup was perceived as too lengthy and lacked clarity about its progress. Users were left unsure of how long the process would take or what stages it involved.
  • Feature Demo Engagement: After profile creation, users wanted to test the app with their own music to evaluate the difference, but the provided demo didn't meet their expectations. They found it unhelpful in showcasing the feature's true value.
  • Volume Adjustment Need: During the initial feature introduction, several users sought the ability to adjust the volume but were unable to do so, which detracted from their overall experience.

Collaboration

Identify, prioritise & refine

Working in real-time with PM, Dev, and Infrastructure teams to build flowcharts, identify technical no-go areas, and explore feasible options from a technical perspective.
