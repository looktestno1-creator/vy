The flow

10 screens from device discovery to first track

The prototype covers the complete onboarding path — starting when the app detects a nearby B&W device, through Bluetooth pairing, and ending with a hi-res track playing in Qobuz.

Users can exit the setup flow at any point and jump straight to music. Tap Start set-up to walk the pairing path, or Do this later to go directly to the Qobuz search experience.

01
Device discovery Modal sheet appears when a nearby Pi8 is detected
02
Product selection User identifies their product from the scan results
03–04
Earbud tap interaction Physical confirmation — tap one earbud to trigger BLE pairing mode
05–06
iOS Bluetooth pairing Native accessory picker hands off to the OS for secure pairing
07
Pairing confirmed Clear success state before transitioning to the music experience
08–09
Qobuz search No account required — search and play hi-res tracks immediately
10
Now playing Full hi-res playback with quality indicator (24-bit · 44.1 kHz)
